# Cached model answers for the real-repository evaluation

These files are cached prompts and answers. They contain excerpts of three
MIT-licensed projects, used at the commits pinned in `benchmarks/real/*.toml`:

* ciscosupportsdk: https://github.com/supermanny81/ciscosupportsdk (MIT)
* okdata-permission-api: https://github.com/oslokommune/okdata-permission-api (MIT, Oslo kommune)
* pydantic-cli: https://github.com/mpkocher/pydantic-cli (MIT)

Copyright stays with their authors. The cache lets anyone re-score the runs
without making model calls.
